BANKING CUSTOMER & MARKETING ANALYTICS: BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT
======================================================================
Total Records Analyzed: 45,211
Dataset Attributes: 18 columns (17 raw source attributes + 1 derived feature 'age_group')
Overall Campaign Subscription Rate: 11.7% (5,289 subscriptions)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BENCHMARK PERFORMANCE
- The dataset indicates an overall term deposit subscription rate of 11.7%.
- Out of 45,211 contacted banking clients, 5,289 agreed to open a term deposit,
  while 39,922 (88.30%) did not subscribe.

2. HIGHEST-PERFORMING CUSTOMER DEMOGRAPHICS
- By Job Category:
  * The analysis shows that 'student' accounts achieved the highest subscription rate at 28.68%,
    followed by 'retired' clients at 22.79%.
  * In contrast, high-volume segments like 'blue-collar' (7.27%) and
    'services' (8.88%) exhibited below-average conversion rates.
- By Age Bracket (Engineered Feature 'age_group'):
  * Clients aged 66+ exhibited the highest subscription rate at 42.61%, followed by the 18-25 cohort (23.95%).
  * The primary working-age segment (36-45 years) demonstrated the lowest subscription rate at 9.39%.
- By Education Level:
  * Clients holding tertiary education had a subscription rate of 15.01%,
    outperforming secondary (10.56%) and primary education (8.63%).

3. IMPACT OF DEBT & LOAN PRODUCTS
- Housing Loans:
  * The dataset reveals that customers without a housing loan subscribed at 16.7%,
    compared to only 7.7% for clients with an active housing loan.
- Personal Loans:
  * Similarly, customers without personal loans subscribed at 12.66%,
    compared to 6.68% for clients with personal loans.
  * The data indicates that existing debt obligations correlate strongly with lower willingness to commit capital to term deposits.

4. MARKETING COMMUNICATION & CONTACT DYNAMICS
- Communication Channel:
  * Cellular communication yielded a subscription rate of 14.92%,
    compared to telephone (13.42%) and unknown contact methods (4.07%).
- Call Duration Profile:
  * The data shows that customers who subscribed had an average contact duration of 537.29 seconds
    (8.95 minutes, median: 426.00 seconds).
  * In comparison, clients who did not subscribe averaged 221.18 seconds
    (3.69 minutes, median: 164.00 seconds).
  * Calls with subscribing clients were on average 316.1 seconds (+142.9%) longer.
  * (Note: This documents an observed correlation; duration is an outcome of an engaged conversation rather than an exogenous cause).

5. PREVIOUS CAMPAIGN EXPOSURE & HISTORICAL OUTCOMES
  * The analysis shows that when a customer's prior campaign outcome was 'success', the current subscription rate reached 64.73%.
  * By contrast, clients with prior 'failure' converted at 12.61%, and those with unknown/no prior history converted at 9.16%.
  * Prior successful engagement is the single strongest indicator of subscription in this dataset.

6. FINANCIAL PROFILE & BALANCE DISTRIBUTION
  * Subscribed customers (y='yes') held an average balance of EUR 1,804.27 (median: EUR 733.00).
  * Non-subscribed clients (y='no') held an average balance of EUR 1,303.71 (median: EUR 417.00).
  * Subscribed clients held on average EUR 500.55 (+38.4%) higher balances.
